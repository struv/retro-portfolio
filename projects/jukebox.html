    Behind the discs

    This little room runs on HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. Three.js handles the 3D discs through WebGL. The deck itself is styled HTML and CSS, and the browser’s native audio player handles the music.

    The rainbow effect comes from a custom GLSL fragment shader, a small program that colors the disc pixel by pixel on the graphics card. It layers waves of color around the center, fine concentric rings to suggest the CD’s grooves, and a sharp glint that shifts as the disc turns. The silver back gets the full effect; the artwork side gets a softer sheen to keep the cover readable. It’s a stylized approximation of how a real CD catches light.

    To keep things light, the artwork uses compressed WebP images or small SVGs, and songs only load when you choose them. Idle animation pauses when you scroll past the player or switch tabs, and the rendering resolution is capped to ease the load on high-resolution screens.
