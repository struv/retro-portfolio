      About

      At Kids & Teens Medical Group, I delivered software alongside medical administration and Excel reporting. Working with clinic staff and operations teams helped me understand where a new application would help and where an existing process needed to stay.

      I defined requirements, launched tools, demonstrated workflows to regional managers and senior staff, and handled support requests. I used AI coding tools extensively for implementation, while owning delivery and coordinating changes with another developer.

      I earned a B.S. in Computer Science from the University of California, Riverside in August 2024. Outside work, I produce electronic music and experiment with browser-based tools and visualizations.

      I’m also an electronic music producer. I’ve been producing and releasing music since 2013, and I founded the Music Production Club at my high school. Making music remains one of my biggest passions.

      I’ve released music under several names and projects over the years. Some of that earlier work lives on DJ Dextek’s YouTube channel and SoundCloud as struv. These days, I keep most of what I make private.
